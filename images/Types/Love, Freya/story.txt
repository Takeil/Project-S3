<p>
The hum of the fluorescent lights buzzed in the office as I sat hunched over my desk, fingers typing away hours of my life for a few words. The clatter of keys echoed in the screams of printers, conversations, and other keyboards racing against deadlines. Papers and files scattered around.

As I delved into the abyss, my phone disrupted the noise with a harmonious melody. Her name illuminated the screen, a flash of color amid the grayscale of my work. I swiped to answer.

"I'm waiting for you at the bus stop. Aren’t you coming?" She said in a sorrowful tone.

"No, we already talked about this, right?"

"But it’s been weeks since we last saw each other," she pleaded.

"My schedule is hectic; you know how toxic my job is. There are tons of calls and emails to answer everyday."

"But it’s our anniversary today. You didn’t even greet me... you won’t even spend time with me. Do you even miss me? Do you even love me?" Her voice trembled with a mixture of hurt and anger.

"Is this the same argument again…" I sighed.

"Yes. How many times have you promised? You told me that we’ll meet today. I was even looking forward to this day. You told me that we’ll fix this relationship, and yet you--"

"And yet you can’t understand, Freya?! I’m working hard to build a better future. This is my love for you. You know that, right?" I sputtered.

"Who cares about the future? You should care about me, I’m your girlfriend, Ethan!"

"That is sacrifice! You… you’ll always be there; we’ll always make time..."

"Ethan? That’s not how relationships work. You should learn how to balance your priorities…"

"Our future is my priority," I declared.

"And what about me? What about now?"

"You know what, Freya, you’re the only one who makes a fuss about this! We’re not kids anymore! Grow up and realize that we need to strive for a better future. When will you realize that? Love is sacrifice," I asserted.

"Ethan... today’s our anniversary-" Freya's voice wavered.

"We can always celebrate our anniversary anytime! It doesn’t matter when or where, what’s important is that we’re together," I insisted.

"Okay, Ethan, maybe you’re right... maybe I’m the only one who needs fixing," she conceded, her voice laced with resignation.

"I can’t do this right now. I’ll call you later. Bye," I uttered. The weight of the conversation settling on my shoulders as I ended the call, leaving behind a digital silence, as the orchestra of my work began to play again.
</p>

<p>
The sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm glow into the room as I stirred from a dream I couldn't quite remember. The elusive pieces clung to the fringes of my consciousness, leaving me with an unsettling sense of something just out of reach. The only thing I remembered was a face, Freya, framed by the wisps of a fading dream.

I blinked away the remnants of sleep, and a peculiar awareness settled in. Her absence resonated within me, echoing the silent question of where she might be. Yet, it wasn't just her missing presence that unsettled me; it was also the unfamiliarity of my surroundings that tugged at the edges of my mind. Why am I here? This isn't our house; this is mine, or at least, it used to be. These walls held the echoes of my youth, memories I thought were locked away.

The bedroom, with its familiar confines, felt emptier, quieter. Even though it was summer, the air felt cooler here, like the life of this room had long dissipated. I scanned the room, taking in the details of a past I struggled to recognize. The remnants of my childhood lingered in the corners, overshadowed by the stark reality of the present.

My gaze wandered, searching for her in the subtle shadows of the room. The curtains danced gently with the evening breeze, hues of twilight painting the room in amber shades, casting long shadows that stretched like ephemeral fingers across the floor. Through the parted curtains, the world outside seemed to hold its breath, caught between day and night.

A peculiar unease settled in my chest as I stood. Where was she? The question lingered in the air, unanswered. My search continued, I need to find her.

I got up from my bed and started looking around.
</p>
